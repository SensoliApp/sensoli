Sensoli Privacy Policy

Privacy and local data protection

Sensoli is designed to operate locally on your Mac. We make every effort to limit the information used by Sensoli to what is necessary for its operation and to provide a simple and transparent experience.

1. Local data storage

Sensoli operates locally. Data relating to your activity is not sent to a server by the application.

Your data is stored locally on your computer and is used to provide Sensoli's analysis, statistics, recommendations, reports, goals, projects, and other features.

2. Information used by Sensoli

Sensoli uses information necessary to detect and analyze application changes during a session, including:

  • the name of the active application;
  • the application's bundle identifier;
  • the date and time of the application change;
  • your local username;
  • the information required to identify the local session.

Sensoli does not monitor the content of the applications you use. It does not record keystrokes, mouse movements, screenshots, files, or the content displayed inside your applications.

3. Account information

Sensoli stores a local username and the information required to manage your local account.

Usernames are unique within the local Sensoli installation.

4. Passwords

Passwords are securely hashed and are never stored in plain text.

5. Third-party services

Sensoli does not send your activity data to third-party servers. The application is designed to process and store its data locally.

6. Data deletion

You can delete individual sessions or permanently delete your local Sensoli account.

Sensoli also allows you to export your data in JSON and CSV formats.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to Sensoli or its data practices.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data protection, you can contact us at:

sensoli.app@outlook.com
