Sensoli Privacy Policy

Privacy and local data security and protection

Sensoli is design to operate locally on MacOS Sillicon (arm64). We make every effort to limit the information used by Sensoli to what is necessary for its operation. Our goal is to provide a reliable and transparent experience.

Local data storage

Sensoli operates locally. Data relating to your activity is not sent to a specific server by the application. For its functionnment Sensoli create a local database on your computer based on SQLite 3.

Your data is stored locally on your computer and is used to provide Sensoli's analysis, statistics, recommendations, reports, goals, projects, and other features. In order to use Sensoli, you need to accept the access to your library directory.

Information used by Sensoli

Sensoli uses information necessary to detect and analyze application changes during a session. This includes :

  • the name of the active applications
  • the application's bundle identifier
  • the date and time of the application change
  • your local username
  • the information requiered to identify the local session (session identifier)

Sensoli does not monitor the content of the applications you use. It does not record keystrokes, mouse movements, screenshots, files, or the content displayed inside your applications.

3. Account information

Sensoli stores a local username and the information required to manage your local account.

Usernames are unique within the local Sensoli installation.

4. Passwords

For your protection, passwords are securely hashed and are never stored in plain text.

5. Third-party services

As we know, Sensoli does not send your activity data to third-party servers. The application is designed to process and store its data locally.

6. Data deletion

You can delete individual sessions or permanently delete your local Sensoli account.

Sensoli also allows you to export your data in JSON and CSV formats.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to Sensoli or its data practices.
